Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

The short version

OnCharter collects only what it needs to run the app on board: your name and email, your profile, and the messages, photos and videos you choose to share.

  • Only people on your yacht see your information, and only what they're allowed to see.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use it for marketing.
  • You can see, correct or delete your information at any time.

OnCharter is run by Murray Willis, a sole trader in New South Wales, Australia ("OnCharter", "we", "us"). We follow the Australian Privacy Principles. Using OnCharter is also covered by our Terms of Use.

What we collect and why

We don't collect your location, contacts, or anything from your phone you haven't chosen to add. The app asks permission before using your camera or photo library, and only uses them when you add a photo or video.

Who on board can see it

Your information stays within your yacht. People on other yachts never see it.

  • Your name and profile photo: everyone on your yacht.
  • Crew profiles: crew and the captain; guests see the crew profiles the captain switches on for Meet Your Crew.
  • Chats between two people: only those two people.
  • Group chats: the people in that group. The captain is in the groups that guests can message.
  • Photos and videos: only the people you chose when you added them, plus you. The captain can also see and remove photos shared with crew or everyone.
  • Your email address: the captain, so they can manage who is on the yacht.
  • New people: someone who joins the yacht later doesn't see earlier chats, photos or videos.

When you're removed from a yacht, you no longer see its information.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it, and how we protect it

  • Keeping it: we keep your information while you have an account. Chats, photos and videos stay with the yacht until they're deleted by you, the captain or the yacht.
  • Deleting your account: we delete your account and profile. Messages you sent in group chats may stay visible to others in that chat, and photos you shared stay in the album unless you delete them first.
  • Backups: deleted information may stay in our backups for a short time (up to 30 days) before it's gone for good.
  • Security: information is sent over encrypted connections, passwords are stored scrambled, and each yacht's information is walled off so only people on that yacht can reach it. No system is completely secure, so if a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we'll tell the people affected and the Australian privacy regulator.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: you can change your name, photo and crew profile in the app. Ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or to correct anything you can't change yourself.
  • Delete: delete your own photos and videos in the app, or ask us to delete your account.
  • Notifications: turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Camera and photos: OnCharter only asks when you take or add a photo, and you can change this in your phone's settings.

To ask for any of these, email us (below). We'll reply within 30 days. If you're in the EU or UK, you may have extra rights under the GDPR, and we'll respect them.

Children, changes and contact

  • Children: accounts are for people 16 and over. Younger guests may only use OnCharter with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you think a child has given us information without permission, tell us and we'll delete it.
  • Changes: if we change this policy in an important way, we'll tell you in the app first. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
  • Contact: email privacy@oncharter.app, or tap the O·C icon on the home screen and choose Contact.
  • Complaints: tell us first and we'll try to fix it. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
